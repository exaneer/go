How it works

  1. Open Dreamwalk with the button above (phone or computer).
  2. Tap “Sky lantern” in the bottom menu.
  3. Write who it's for and your wish.
  4. Tap “Release” — your lantern rises and becomes a card.
  5. Tap “Send card” and share the link with anyone.

What is Dreamwalk?

A free web space where you walk a Korean palace pond at night in hanbok and release wish lanterns with people around the world. You can also float lotus lanterns, try the folk game jwibulnori (fire spinning), and start a lantern festival that fills the sky. Available in 15 languages.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no app, no payment — just open the link.

Can the other person reply?

Yes. Whoever opens your card can release a thank-you lantern back to you.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It runs in any modern mobile or desktop browser.

Is it a real Korean festival?

It's a creative space inspired by Korean palaces and Korea's lantern traditions such as Yeondeunghoe — not a recreation of a specific event.
